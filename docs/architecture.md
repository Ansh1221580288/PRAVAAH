# PRAVAAH System Architecture

## 1. DATA SOURCES

- Weather / Radar
- Rainfall
- Satellite Imagery
- River / Water Level
- Soil Moisture
- DEM / Terrain / Slope
- Land Use
- Roads / Infrastructure
- Historical Disasters
- Citizen / Field Reports

## 2. HAZARD INTELLIGENCE

- Flood Prediction
- Flash-Flood Prediction
- Landslide Risk Prediction
- Rainfall / Weather Nowcasting
- Hazard Probability & Risk Score

## 3. IMPACT INTELLIGENCE

- Inundation / Hazard Extent
- Population Exposed
- Vulnerable Population
- Roads / Bridges Affected
- Hospitals / Schools / Critical Infrastructure
- High-Priority / Red Zones
- Resource Requirement Estimation

## 4A. AUTHORITY CONSOLE

- Live Risk Map
- Priority Sectors
- Affected Population
- Resource Allocation
- Rescue Planning
- Road Closures
- Situation Monitoring

## 4B. CITIZEN APPLICATION

- Early Warning
- Voice Alert
- Safe Route
- Safe Shelter
- SOS
- Citizen Reporting
- Local Language

## 5. TWO-WAY FEEDBACK LOOP

- Citizen Reports
- Rescue Team Updates
- Water-Level Reports
- Road Blockage Reports
- Damage Reports
- Authority Decisions

## Continuous Risk Update

Feedback from citizens, rescue teams, field observations, and authorities
is used to continuously update the Hazard Intelligence and Impact Intelligence.
